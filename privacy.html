Privacy

Who is responsible

Fausto Melchiorre runs oneuro. Contact: faustobase@hotmail.com. Website: faustomelchiorre.com.

What we collect

What you enter in the signup form: your email address, whether you build apps or want to try them, your answer about credits, and anything you choose to tell us about your app.

Why, and on what basis

To email you about oneuro: news about the launch and, for makers, an invitation to share your app. We do this because you asked us to by joining the list (consent).

Who processes it

The signup form is run by Tally BV, based in Belgium, which collects and stores your answers for us (Tally’s privacy policy). If we send emails through another service, we will name it here. Our web host, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States), may log technical data such as your IP address.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe from any email, or write to us to see, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your national data protection authority.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The signup form opens on Tally’s own site, where Tally’s policies apply.
